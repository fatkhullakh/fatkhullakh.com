## What it is

Shops end the day with food they can't sell. Saqla lets them list it at a discount, and lets people nearby buy it instead of it going to waste.

## What I built

- A backend with role-based access for shops, customers and admins.
- Payment integration with Payme and Click, two payment providers in Uzbekistan, including fiscalization of receipts.
- Hosting and deployment on AWS.

## Outcome

It did not turn into a business that made money. It was my first time building a product with real payments and real users, and it's where I learned most of what I know about shipping software end to end.
